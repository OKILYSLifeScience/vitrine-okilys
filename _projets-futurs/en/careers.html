The profiles we recruit

Senior Clinical Research Associate (Senior CRA)

The CRA carries out site follow-up, on-site or remote visits (Qualification, Site Initiation, Monitoring, Close-out, Audit) and reports to Clinical Project Managers or the CRA Lead.

The Senior CRA works autonomously. They communicate spontaneously and regularly with the project team (reporting, escalating issues, resolving actions and CAPAs, adverse event follow-up, treatment management, liaising with medical monitors and trial vendors,…). They build a trusted relationship with investigator sites to maintain a good level of training among investigator teams, ensure patient safety and protocol compliance, and develop an effective strategy for patient recruitment and retention in the studies they oversee.

Requirements:

  • Minimum 2 years' experience
  • ICH-GCP(R2) training
  • Fluent written and spoken English

Clinical Project Manager (CPM)

CPMs work closely with the project's Medical and Scientific team, the Sponsor, Vendors and the CRA teams (and CRA Lead where applicable) under their responsibility. They handle the design, updating and follow-up of study documents and their archiving in the eTMF, as well as follow-up of actions by operational teams, review of site visit reports (Qualification, Initiation, Monitoring, Close-out). They oversee the study's budget follow-up.

Requirements:

  • Minimum 2 years' experience
  • ICH-GCP(R2) training
  • Fluent written and spoken English

Study-Coordinators (SC) & Clinical Trial Technicians (TEC)

SCs support investigator teams on-site in running trials: patient pre-screening, preparing patient visits, coordinating clinical staff, data entry in Case Report Forms (CRF) and e-CRF (TEC activity), follow-up of Adverse Events (serious and non-serious) and concomitant treatments. They create tools and procedures from the protocol to ensure patient visits and trial logistics comply with protocol and regulatory requirements.

Requirements:

  • Minimum 1 year's experience
  • ICH-GCP(R2) training
  • Ability to read and understand written and spoken English

Study Start-Up Specialist (SSU Specialist)

The SSU Specialist prepares the necessary documents (Consent forms, Patient documents, Protocol and French protocol summary,…) for regulatory submissions to French authorities (ANSM, CPP, CNIL, CNOM). They advise the project team on regulatory submission strategy and expected timelines for obtaining the various approvals. They handle submissions, communication and follow-up with these authorities as well as reporting to the project team throughout the process until approvals are obtained.

They advise the project team on contracting strategy with French sites, draft the Convention Unique and its Annexes for the Coordinating and Associated Sites, handle sending them to the relevant sites as well as negotiation between sites and the sponsor. They are the sites' privileged point of contact throughout the start-up process, notably preparing and collecting the documents required to obtain the green light for study start-up. They also archive all these documents in the study's Trial Master File (TMF) and report to the Start-Up and Project teams.

Requirements:

  • Minimum 2 years' experience
  • ICH-GCP(R2) training
  • Fluent written and spoken English
  • Knowledge of regulations, authorities and contract negotiation in France and/or Belgium and/or Switzerland
