Conventional drugs and advanced therapies: two paces, one journey

Advanced therapy medicinal products (ATMPs) — gene therapy, cell therapy, tissue engineering (Regulation (EC) No 1394/2007) — follow the same path as a conventional drug, in a compressed version: few eligible patients (rare diseases, refractory cancers), phases I/II often combined, a marketing authorisation that is necessarily centralised at the EMA (CAT committee), and patient follow-up for up to 15 years. Many also fall under GMO regulations (gene therapies, CAR-T), which add their own authorisations.

Regulatory frameworks

  • WorldwideDeclaration of Helsinki · ICH E6(R3) good clinical practice, applicable in Europe since July 2025 · phases I-IV and CTD dossier harmonised by ICH · public trial registration (WHO principle) · in the United States: IND application to the FDA, ethics review by IRBs
  • European UnionRegulation (EU) No 536/2014, applicable since January 2022 · single submission through the CTIS portal, coordinated assessment between Member States · centralised marketing authorisation (European Commission on EMA opinion) or national
  • FranceLoi Jardé — RIPH 1 · double green light: ANSM authorisation + favourable opinion from an ethics committee (CPP), through CTIS · written consent mandatory
  • BelgiumLaw of 7 May 2017 on clinical trials of medicinal products for human use (implementing Regulation 536/2014) · joint assessment by the FAMHP and an accredited ethics committee, the FAMHP being the national contact point · written consent
