OKILYS suite

Tools born in the field of clinical trials

OKILYS Suite is the eClinical range edited by OKILYS Life Science: tools born from cross-cutting field expertise — different roles, different companies, different studies — serving the teams who move studies forward day after day — biotechs, sponsors, CROs and hospitals — with the means and headcount of small organisations.

Discover OKILYS CTMS OKILYS eTMF (coming soon)

Why these tools exist

Twelve years holding every position of the chain — site coordination, monitoring, start-up and contracts, study management, oversight — showed the same thing everywhere: studies rarely stall on the science — almost always on the organisation and on scattered communication, written and spoken alike: the same information to update manually in several places, and someone to remember to inform every time.

The Excel tracker that drifts

Site list, follow-up actions, CRA oversight, training: one file per topic, shared by e-mail or SharePoint, with its off-list entries, lost rows and wrong dates. Every progress meeting started with a reconciliation. And a file that stays editable by everyone means a weak audit trail — and the permanent risk of changing something without meaning to.

The same information typed many times

A visit date copied into the site record, the tracker, the weekly-call minutes, the checklists and the visit reports. So many chances to get it wrong, to miss an update — and to generate findings.

Tools hardly accessible to small organisations

Market platforms are built for large companies: licences, deployments and training beyond the reach of small organisations running a few studies with a limited team.

A conviction

A useful tool follows the team's real work without scattering its energy: what is entered once propagates everywhere, what is done is traced, what is missing is visible before it costs time, data or patients.
