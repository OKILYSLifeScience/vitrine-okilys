OKILYS suite · Clinical trial management

OKILYS CTMS — the CTMS for small clinical teams

A CTMS designed for biotechs, sponsors and CROs that have no time to babysit Excel files. OKILYS CTMS centralises site feasibility, selection and activation, visits, electronically signed reports, follow-up actions and site documents. You only switch on the modules you need, and your data is handed back to you at any time.

Let's talk about your study

A 30-minute demonstration on your own use cases · Client access: ctms.okilys.com ↗

Why replace Excel trackers?

Invisible errors

Free-text entries, deleted rows, duplicates, wrong dates: a shared tracker always ends up diverging from what actually happens on site.

A single source of truth

Every piece of information is entered once (visit, decision, date) and appears everywhere it is displayed, with no re-typing.

ALCOA+ traceability

Who created, changed, closed, signed, and when: full audit trail, report versions, electronic signatures.

Your data stays yours

Nightly backups, a data package per client, full export at the end of the partnership. Dedicated hosting, nothing shared between clients.

Modules on demand

For each client and each study, switch on only the building blocks you need. The codes give the sponsor, the CRO and the CRAs a shared language.

A1 · A2

Site life cycle

From pre-identification to selection (CDA, feasibility questionnaire, reminders, SSV, decision), then from selection to activation (regulatory, ethics, contract, SIV, activation). Dashboard of both phases, per country. Greenlight checklist built automatically and signed before each site is initiated.

B1 · B4

Site visits

SSV, SIV, SMV, COV: scheduling, confirmation letter, attendance sheet, questionnaire-based report, follow-up letter. OKILYS templates adaptable per client and per study. Reports signed by the writer, then by a reviewer (dedicated role available).

C

Follow-up actions

Actions at study, country or site level, automatic numbering, H/M/L/O levels, assignment to a person, traced closure, dated follow-ups, Excel and PDF exports.

D1 · D3

Submissions and contracts

Regulatory follow-up, ethics committee, contract and budget per site, dates inherited into the site life cycle.

E · F

Reporting and settings

Dashboards suggested by role and study phase, fully configurable pivot tables, charts (actual vs planned recruitment, planned vs actual activation), indicators, extractions; study team, templates, audit log, trash.

G1 · G4

Standalone tools

Automated report review, CRA call tracking, site document filing, study Q&A log: usable on their own, without the rest of the CTMS.

H

Study documents, training and news

Every version of the study documents, globally and per country, updated automatically from submissions; training assigned by role and country, reading certified and traced, team training status extracted in one click; study news published automatically.

I

eTMF

Trial Master File structured per the TMF Reference Model: collected documents, study document versions, submissions, contracts, greenlights and signed reports filed automatically as drafts in the right section, then verified; completeness per zone, country and site; ISF review from monitoring visits; indexed ZIP export.

J

Planning and resources

Study plan (phases, milestones, tasks) with Gantt, actual milestone dates read from the CTMS, resources, CRA capacity and workload; configurable by the project lead.

K

Safety and IMP

SAE log fed from monitoring visits, reconciled with the listing of the client's chosen eCRF; IMP accountability review at every visit.

Four tools you can adopt on their own

Automated report review

Consistency checks on every report item (missing answers, unjustified N/A, missing follow-up actions, insufficient comments, missing versions and dates) plus coverage checks against the OKILYS checklist. Also works on your existing Word or PDF reports.

CRA call tracking

The weekly CTM/CPL – CRA call grid, from selection to close-out: one column per call, one row per site, statuses inherited automatically and highlighted when they changed since the last call. CRA unavailable? An update request reaches them as a 5-business-day task. Excel export in the format your teams already know.

Site documents

Upload of collected documents (CVs, licences, GCP, DoI…) straight from the visit report, naming per the study convention, versions, fingerprints, ZIP export and hand-back.

Q&A log

Every question from sites, CRAs or the sponsor and every study-team answer, numbered, sorted by category and country. Excel export for the TMF or the investigator meeting.

Security and compliance, by design

Access

Named accounts, mandatory two-factor authentication, per-study roles (CPL/CTM, CRA, viewer), access periods, immediate revocation.

Electronic signatures

Reports finalised with two advanced electronic signatures (writer then reviewer): re-authentication, content fingerprint, timestamp, verifiable manifest.

Audit trail

Every creation, change, closure, download and signature is logged (who, when, before / after). Soft deletion with a trash bin.

Confidentiality

Your data serves nothing but your study. No third-party services; hosted in France.
