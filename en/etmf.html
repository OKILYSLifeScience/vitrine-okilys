OKILYS suite · Trial Master File

OKILYS eTMF — the TMF that fills itself during the study, not after coming soon

An electronic Trial Master File structured per the TMF Reference Model and connected to OKILYS CTMS: documents produced during the study are filed automatically as drafts in the right section, your teams verify them, and completeness can be read at any time — instead of the painful end-of-study reconstruction.

Be notified at launch

OKILYS eTMF is under development — tell us about your needs now, they will shape the tool.

What OKILYS eTMF will do

Automatic filings

Documents collected from sites, study document versions, regulatory and ethics submissions, contracts, greenlight checklists and signed visit reports: every record produced in the CTMS lands as a draft in the right section of the TMF, at the right level (trial, country, site).

Human verification

Nothing is finalised on its own: the country CRA or the project lead is notified, verifies each filing and validates it — or rejects it. Manual filings complete the file.

Continuous completeness

Expected artifacts configured per study, completeness measured per zone, country and site: you know what is missing at any time, without waiting for the audit.

Connected ISF/PSF review

From monitoring visits, the CRA ticks off what was reviewed in the investigator site file, with an automatic cross-check against the eTMF, and uploads the collected documents — filed straight in as drafts.

Aligned with industry conventions

TMF Reference Model zones / sections / artifacts, Trial / Country / Site levels, ICH E6(R3) expectations. Draft / Final / Superseded statuses, traced versions.

Full hand-back

Indexed ZIP export (folders per zone, section, artifact and level, plus a CSV index): the TMF is handed to the sponsor or the auditor in one gesture, during the study and at close-out.

Designed with OKILYS CTMS from day one

OKILYS eTMF is the natural extension of OKILYS CTMS: because the CTMS already traces visits, signed reports, submissions and site documents, the TMF can build itself with no double data entry. It will be offered as a CTMS module, then as a dedicated site.
