Our expertise

here to support you

✍️

Study design and study documents

We draw on our hands-on experience of running clinical trials in France to advise you on writing your clinical research protocols and study documents.

📁

Study start-up activities

We handle your regulatory submissions, the preparation and negotiation of contracts with investigator sites, and the collection of the administrative documents required to start a clinical trial in France, French-speaking Belgium and French-speaking Switzerland.

👥

Project management

We support you with budget follow-up and the coordination of your clinical study activities, as well as the supervision of operational teams.

🔎

Investigator site monitoring

OKILYS carries out all site visits, from pre-selection to close-out, across a wide range of therapeutic areas — including remote follow-up of sites, enrolment and patient safety.

🤝

Investigator site support

We support investigator teams by taking on activities ranging from administrative tasks to patient appointment scheduling, help with identifying potential patients, data entry, and preparing for and assisting monitors during follow-up visits or audits.

🛡️

Clinical Oversight

We provide quality and compliance supervision of external vendors (CROs, service providers) on behalf of sponsors, with rigorous tracking of performance indicators and clinical oversight plans.

Our expertise in detail

How a clinical study unfolds

From feasibility to close-out

The main stages that structure a clinical study, from initial scoping to the final report — OKILYS steps in wherever each study needs it.

Stage 1

Feasibility & scoping

Submission strategy based on the trial type and the applicable regulations (EU, national).

Stage 2

Start-up & activation

Regulatory and ethics submissions, contract and budget negotiation, Greenlight through to activation.

Stage 3

Monitoring & supervision

Monitoring, quality follow-up, risk management and CAPA, KPI/Metrics reporting to stakeholders.

Stage 4

Close-out & wrap-up

Site close-out, performance review and lessons learned for future studies.
