OKILYS Life Science

Together, let's shape the health of future generations!

OKILYS Life Science is a young and dynamic company with an innovative outlook on human health, supporting clinical research stakeholders in the success of their clinical trials.

Based in France, OKILYS Life Science works across clinical research, studies and evaluations (drugs, medical devices, real-world practice, collected data) as well as Non-Drug Interventions (NDI) of high scientific and therapeutic quality, while giving people back their role as actors in their own health.

Our mission?

  • Support private and public sector stakeholders in designing, preparing and carrying out their clinical research projects.
  • Maintain a high standard of scientific and regulatory rigour, with constant attention to patient safety and wellbeing.

At OKILYS Life Science, methodological rigour and careful listening to each project's needs are at the heart of every mission, with one constant goal: improving the healthcare offered to people.

Our expertise

to support you

✍️

Study design and documents

We draw on our experience of running clinical trials in France to advise you on drafting your clinical research protocols and study documents.

📁

Study start-up activities

We handle your regulatory submissions, the preparation and negotiation of contracts with investigator sites, and the collection of administrative documents needed to start a clinical trial in France, French-speaking Belgium and French-speaking Switzerland.

👥

Project management

We support you with budget follow-up and coordination of your clinical research project activities, as well as the oversight of operational teams.

🔎

Investigator site monitoring

OKILYS handles all site visits, from pre-selection to close-out, across varied therapeutic areas, including remote monitoring of sites, enrolment and patient safety.

🤝

Support to investigator sites

We support investigator teams by handling activities ranging from administrative tasks to scheduling patient appointments, helping identify potential patients, data entry, and preparing and assisting monitors during monitoring visits or audits.

🛡️

Clinical Oversight

We provide quality oversight and compliance monitoring of external providers (CROs, vendors) on behalf of sponsors, with rigorous tracking of performance indicators and clinical oversight plans.
