About us

Qualified expertise, tailored to each study

Clinical development

From molecule to medicine

Clinical studies are part of a long, strictly regulated development process that takes a candidate molecule all the way to the patient.

Preclinical research

Screening of thousands of molecules · in vitro and in vivo studies

Phase I

Safety and tolerability · a few dozen healthy volunteers

Phase II

Efficacy and optimal dose · a few hundred patients

Phase III

Large-scale confirmation · thousands of patients, multicenter

MA

Marketing Authorisation · EMA / ANSM

Phase IV

Pharmacovigilance and real-world data, after launch

⏱ A journey of roughly 10 to 15 years overall

Our expertise, from design to close-out

Each bar shows the study stages covered by an OKILYS field of intervention.

1. Design

Strategic advice based on trial type, protocol & synopsis, ICF: writing, country adaptation, French translation review (FR, BE, CH, LU…), eCRF, CTMS, patient-facing tools

2. Site selection

Country and investigator site selection: feasibility, selection visits

3. Preparation

Collection of site essential documents, contract & budget negotiation, submission package preparation

4. Submissions

Submission strategy based on trial type · EU Regulation: CTIS Part II · National frameworks: ANSM & CPP (FR), FAMHP & Ethics Committees (BE) — initial & amendments, up to Greenlight (approval + signed contract)

5. Site initiation

Initiation visits, team training, site activation, first patient in

6. Conduct & monitoring

Patient recruitment, monitoring & recruitment-boosting visits, close communication & escalation, data quality, eTMF, risk & deviation management

7. Close-out

Site close-out visits, final query resolution, drug & material returns, TMF/eTMF reconciliation & archiving, study wrap-up

The founder

Lydie Parsus, founder of OKILYS

🌳 Photo of Lydie to be added

With a strong scientific background, Lydie Parsus began her clinical research career in the hospital environment, first as a Clinical Research Technician and then as a Study Coordinator, before becoming a monitoring CRA. There she built close working relationships with physicians, nurses, patients and the teams running clinical trials in the field.

Her signature: full trial oversight from start-up to close-out, across all phases — combining regulatory expertise, site engagement, operational oversight and team coordination, both locally and internationally.

For more than 12 years, she has made the deliberate choice to explore the various roles that make up the clinical research ecosystem: Clinical Research Technician and CRA in public hospitals, Senior CRA/Site Manager, Lead CRA, SSU/RSS Specialist, Clinical Project Manager, and now consultant through her company OKILYS Life Science. She has worked in a wide range of environments — hospital DRCIs, laboratories, small, medium and large CROs — which has given her a global perspective on clinical research and real operational agility, moving naturally from one role to another depending on the needs of the project or the team.

She has developed recognised expertise in contract and budget negotiation with investigator sites (Convention Unique, direct and tripartite contracts, Pharma.be model), managing up to thirty contracts in parallel across several countries — a rigour that now underpins the OKILYS approach.

This experience goes hand in hand with training and mentoring junior CRAs, and with her work as a Clinical Oversight Manager, providing quality supervision and performance tracking of vendors (CROs, service providers) on behalf of sponsors. Her experience spans a wide range of indications, from rare diseases (cystic fibrosis, epidermolysis bullosa, Cushing's syndrome…) to more common conditions (cardiovascular, digestive, cancers…), always with the same guiding principle: improving the healthcare offered to people.

She now aims to gradually move into roles with broader responsibilities, combining team coordination, operational oversight and strategic contribution — while remaining adaptable to the needs of both projects and teams, on human and organisational levels.

What she brings

Five facets of a single expertise

🧾

From submission planning to dossier delivery

She defines the optimal submission strategy based on trial type and the applicable EU and national regulations. She prepares and coordinates submission packages (regulatory and ethics) for both initial submissions and substantial amendments — with hands-on experience of ANSM, CPP, CTIS, CNIL/HDH (France) and FAMHP/local Ethics Committees (Belgium).

🚦

Efficient site management before FPI

She leads feasibility assessments, SSU activities and essential document collection. She negotiates contracts and budgets with French and Belgian sites, adapting to institutional, legal and financial frameworks (Pharma.be, Convention Unique, ARS/CNOM) — with one constant focus: streamlining timelines while ensuring full compliance from the start.

🔎

From initiation to close-out, on-site or remotely

She conducts site initiation, monitoring and close-out visits across various models: on-site, remote, RBM or DCT. She tracks data quality, protocol adherence and timelines with investigators and site staff — her field experience enables her to build strong site relationships and anticipate risks.

🤝

Bridging operations and leadership

She acts as Clinical Trial Lead or operational coordinator across multiple countries (France, Belgium, Germany, Netherlands). She manages priorities, supports junior CRAs, trains new staff and ensures smooth collaboration between sponsor, CRO and site teams — her dual view, strategic and operational, makes her a reliable team anchor.

🎯

From expert input to tailored support

She supports sponsors and CROs with regulatory, methodological and operational advice, especially for non-standard or early-phase trials. She adapts quickly to various roles (SSU, Senior CRA, CTL) and contexts (local or international, fully remote or hybrid) — bringing autonomy, precision and value, even in demanding environments.
