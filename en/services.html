OKILYS expertise

Are you a Pharmaceutical Laboratory, a Biotech, a CRO, a Hospital,… looking for qualified clinical trial expertise, matched to the needs of your current or upcoming projects?

OKILYS assesses your needs and mobilises the expertise best suited to your projects: investigator site management and follow-up, Start-Up and Regulatory activities, clinical trial coordination (Clinical Trial Lead/Manager), quality oversight of vendors (Clinical Oversight),…

OKILYS ensures rigorous follow-up of every engagement, from scoping to close-out.

Okilys Life Science

Clinical trial management and monitoring

Clinical Research Associates (CRAs): they carry out site follow-up, on-site or remote visits (Qualification, Site Initiation, Monitoring, Close-out, Audit) and reporting to project teams.

🖼️Photo

Okilys Life Science

Investigator Site Coordination

Clinical Trial Technicians (TECs) and Study-Coordinators (SCs): they support investigator teams on-site in running the trial: patient pre-screening, preparing patient visits, coordinating clinical staff, data entry in CRFs and e-CRFs, follow-up of Adverse Events (serious and non-serious) and concomitant treatments. They create tools and procedures from the protocol to ensure patient visits and trial logistics comply with protocol and regulatory requirements.

🖼️Photo

Okilys - Investigator Site Coordination

E-tools design for studies

Drawing on our experience with investigator sites and patients, as well as with CROs, vendors and various sponsors, we can support you in designing the electronic tools used in clinical trials:

  • CTMS
  • e-CRF
  • Patient-facing tools
🖼️Photo

Okilys Life Science

Start-Up and Regulatory Activities

Study Start-Up and regulatory specialists (SSU/RSU Specialist) – France, French-speaking Belgium and French-speaking Switzerland: they carry out submissions to local authorities, transparency declarations, follow-up of regulatory activities, contract creation and negotiation with sites (national or other supplied templates, Coordinating and Associated Sites), archiving in the study's TMF (Trial Master File), preparation of study documents and reporting to Start-Up and Project teams.

🖼️Photo

Okilys - Investigator Site Coordination

Project Coordination

Project Coordinators or CRA Leads or Clinical Trial Leads: they work closely with the Project Manager, the Sponsor, Vendors and the CRA teams under their responsibility. They handle the design, updating and follow-up of study documents and their archiving in the TMF (Trial Master File), as well as follow-up of actions by operational teams, review of site visit reports (Qualification, Initiation, Monitoring, Close-out). They contribute to the study's budget follow-up.
