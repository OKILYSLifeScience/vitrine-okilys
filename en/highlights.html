Start-Up driven end to end for 11 sites

RSS & Contract Specialist · 2024-2025

On a large-scale study under EU-CTR (phases I-II, oncology and ophthalmology): drove the entire Start-Up activity through to delivery — full preparation and submissions (steps 3 and 4 of our journey), essential documents, contracts and budgets, through to activation of the 11 sites within timelines.

Resolving a budget impasse in oncology

SSU & Contract Specialist · 2023-2024

On a complex project (Convention Unique, EU regulations), 50% of the 16 sites refused the sponsor-approved budget, and site activation was already delayed by this deadlock when the file was handed over. Revised the budget grid for 8 sites, integrated protocol amendment corrections, renegotiated with all parties: the situation was fully untangled and the amended contracts signed within a timeframe compatible with the study timeline.

Turnkey CNIL declaration strategy

SSU & Contract Specialist · 2023

Developed and implemented the CNIL reference methodologies declaration strategy, consistent with the delegation of authority between CRO and sponsor — including a step-by-step guidance document enabling SSU colleagues to perform submissions in line with regulatory and contractual requirements.

Regulatory qualification of a data-only study

SSU & Contract Specialist · 2021

For a French study involving patient data only, with no internal expertise available: regulatory research, discussions with the French authorities to confirm the RNIPH classification, and guidance to the sponsor and the team — securing the regulatory pathway and Health Data Hub compliance.
