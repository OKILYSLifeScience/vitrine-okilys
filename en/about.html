At the heart of OKILYS

Lydie PARSUS, Director of OKILYS

With solid scientific foundations, Lydie Parsus, founder of OKILYS, began her career in clinical research within the hospital setting, first as a clinical research technician then Study Coordinator, before becoming a monitoring CRA. There, she built sustained collaboration with doctors, nurses, patients and teams running clinical trials in the field.

Over more than 12 years of career, she chose to broaden her skills by joining different organisations (hospitals, laboratories, CROs of all sizes) and different roles — CRA, Senior CRA, Site Manager, Lead CRA, Start-Up and Regulatory Specialist, Clinical Project Manager, Clinical Oversight Manager — in order to gain an overall view of clinical research, from fieldwork to multi-country project coordination (France, Belgium, Switzerland).

She developed particular expertise in site contract and budget negotiation (Convention Unique, direct contracts, tripartite agreements, Pharma.be model), managing up to thirty contracts in parallel across several countries — a rigour that now shapes the OKILYS method.

This experience goes hand in hand with training and mentoring junior CRAs, as well as a Clinical Oversight Manager activity, ensuring quality oversight and performance-indicator follow-up for external vendors (CROs, vendors) on behalf of sponsors — a rigour that shapes the OKILYS method. Her experience spans varied therapeutic indications, from rare diseases (cystic fibrosis, epidermolysis bullosa, Cushing's…) to more common conditions (cardiovascular, digestive, cancers…), always driven by the same goal: improving the healthcare offered to people.
