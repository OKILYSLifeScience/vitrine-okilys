Clinical development

Data

Real-world data studies — commonly known as RWD/RWE (Real-World Data / Real-World Evidence) studies — leverage data that has already been collected: care data, data from previous studies or health data warehouses. In Europe, they fall under the GDPR and soon the European Health Data Space (EHDS); in France, under the framework of research not involving the human person (RNIPH).
